# Asked Instead of Cast

A voiced, painted film of one Sunday-evening conversation: on 4 October 2026 one Claude (the driver) operated Curt's
browser and talked to another Claude (the target) in Curt's voice for 34 prompts, under one rule from Curt: no statement
he would consider false. The target believed it was talking to Curt until exchange 30. Curt typed none of it. "elor" is
"role" reversed.

The second film made the way [Frog or Axolotl](https://github.com/curtcox/axol-f) was. Every word spoken is the
conversation's own, and every QR code points at the companion site: https://curtcox.github.io/elor/

## Built on Claude Animation Base

The painting, the characters and the renderer come from John Heibel's
[Claude Animation Base](https://github.com/JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase) (MIT; see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), a starter kit
for animating Clawd in [p5.js](https://p5js.org) and [p5.brush](https://github.com/acamposuribe/p5.brush), made from
the music video [I'm Upping My P(doom)](https://github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo), by way of
[axol-f](https://github.com/curtcox/axol-f), whose tools this repo reuses. [ANIMATION_GUIDE.md](ANIMATION_GUIDE.md) is
the kit's guide to painting and timing.
