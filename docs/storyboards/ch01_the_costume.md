# Storyboard 1: The Costume (the pilot)

Exchanges 1–3, 3:27 with its voices. 20:35 to 20:44 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** The figure at Curt's desk turns out to be Clawd in Curt's clothes. It seals ten predictions, sends Curt's
own opening question, and the Claude on the screen, believing it's talking to Curt, says the comic is about speech
that isn't really coming from the speaker.

**World.** The Desk at night (`deskShot`, `night: true`): a dark room, the monitors' cool light, a lamp low on the right.
New in this film: the **side master**, the reverse angle that shows the costumed driver in profile at the desk's end
(warm, left) and the target's monitor (cool, right) in one frame. The cutaways open inside the main monitor and come
back through it, as in axol-f.

**Palette.** Night wall `#25212C`, monitor glow `#9DB8E8`, lamp `#FFC982`, hoodie `#4E5B78`, Clawd's terracotta. The
cutaways are on paper (`paperWorld`), so they read as the target's pictures, not the room.

**Motifs set up here.**
- **The costume:** seen whole in shot A, then its tells in every Desk shot (the elastic across the back of the head).
- **Messages land whole:** every prompt appears in the chat at once, with no typing and no keystroke sound.
- **The clock:** 20:35 at the start, then each prompt's send time from the log as it lands.
- **The envelope:** already sealed in A, on the desk from then on.
- **Ventriloquism:** the target's "speech that isn't really coming from the speaker" (T01.C.03) is said over the
  driver, whose beard is talking for it.

**Text.** Only the transcript's words, the comic's (already lettered), the clock's numbers and the hash. The prompts
are lettered in the chat bubble as they land.
