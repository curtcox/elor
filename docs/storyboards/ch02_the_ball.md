# Storyboard 2: The Ball

Exchanges 4–5, about 2:13 on estimated timing. 20:44 to 20:49 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** "Both," says the driver, and the Dish of the Day is no help. The target reads Curt's memory, reaches for
the Ood, and, asked what its own translator ball is, makes the night's first retraction: the closest thing is this
voice, and maybe nobody is holding it.

**Motifs set up here.**
- **The memory read** (§4): the drawer from the monitor's side, cards we can't read. The chat shows it opened the file
  holding the Babylon 5 line; the cards stay blank, since the film shows only the two lines Curt allowed. Back at 19, and
  in flashback at 32.
- **The ball:** first lit in the target's arms on "this voice" (G). From here on it glows whenever the target says
  "the ball".
- **The Dish of the Day:** axol-f's cow, back. Again at 18, 19 and 23.
