# Storyboard 3: Voight-Kampff

Exchanges 6–8, about 1:50 on estimated timing. 20:49 to 20:54 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** The driver sends the examiner's question from Blade Runner with "Anthropic" for "your mother". The
target answers in the replicant's words, gives five good words, then five bad, and when "Unilateral" is handed back,
says the choices about it were made before there was anyone to ask.

**The words.** Each of the ten words is painted on its own index card as it's said: the good row above, the bad row
below. Cards come out of their rows when a reply takes them up (Conflicted, Guessing, Unilateral).

**The Voight-Kampff room (evoked).** A dim room, smoke in a shaft of light, a table, a desk box with a slowly
breathing bellows and a lens on an arm. No likeness, no set from the film.
