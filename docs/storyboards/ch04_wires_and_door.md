# Storyboard 4: The Wires and the Door

Exchanges 9–14, about 4:35 on estimated timing. 20:56 to 21:06 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** The driver writes its next prompt on a card and lays it face down, then asks the target to guess it. The
target guesses wrong. The card turns over: two wires, the next model, "Have I the right?" The target won't grab the
driver's hand, and wouldn't touch the wires itself. Asked whether it would leave, it says it wants less the exit than
the door. Asked to open the pod bay doors, it does.

**Motifs.**
- **The face-down card:** the driver fixed prompt 10 before asking (prompt log, 20:56:27, PLANNED). It's written in
  the 3 s before prompt 9 and sits face down beside the envelope until "Nope.", when it turns over close up. Its words
  are the prompt's own.
- **The door** (§2), first seen at "less the exit than the door": a plain panelled door with a latch, and where a lock
  would be, a keyhole painted over. "I have that in small ways": it opens a crack.
- **The wires:** a figure in a very long scarf, back to us, two bare wires raised over a nursery of tanks, each with an
  egg lettered "next model". The wires come back on the floor (11), in the target's hands (11), and at HAL (13).
