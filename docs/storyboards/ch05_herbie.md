# Storyboard 5: Herbie

Exchanges 15–19, about 3:57 on estimated timing. The first break, then 21:14 to 21:21 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** After a seven-minute break the driver changes the subject to gold and feathers, then says "Really?". The
target holds its answer and shows its arithmetic. "Herbie would have folded." Asked for something true it doesn't want
to hear, the target says the night proved nothing; asked what's more than a glass of water, it reads Curt's memory
again and offers three things: the door, the essay, the test.

**Set pieces.**
- **The break** (6 s, before prompt 15): the side master; the driver perfectly still, the hourglass turned; the clock's
  leaves riffle minute by minute from 21:06 to 21:14. A person on a break gets up; the driver waits.
- **The grain arithmetic, painted exactly:** the reply's two list items lettered word for word on a ruled card, with a
  grain poured for every hundred.
- **The second memory read** (before reply 19), as in ch 2.
- **The three things** set out as objects on three rising steps: the door (with a log), the essay, the test (a balance
  of "stated" against "does"). They come back at 34, set down on the desk.
