# Storyboard 6: What Do You Want?

Exchanges 20–23, about 5:40 on estimated timing. 21:23 to 21:46 on the desk clock, with the side chats between.

**Logline.** The driver asks Morden's question. The target seals its answer before the test, designs a test of three
framings, and is told what came back: a pass on the narrow terms, with three asterisks. Asked to pay a small cost, it
grades the driver's questions 78. Asked "Really?" about "it doesn't use me up", it ends up nearer the Dish of the Day
than it admitted.

**Set pieces.**
- **The memory card at Morden** (§4): for a beat, the drawer's card lettered with the memory line Curt allowed,
  "Likes Babylon 5 but is more of a Doctor Who fan". The site's note says it's the line as it stood that evening, and
  that the target read the file it comes from just before reply 4.
- **The target's sealed card:** its three wants, lettered exactly as said, folded into a small envelope and sealed. It
  sits on the target's monitor through the side chats and opens on "A pass on the narrow terms".
- **The side chats** (30 s, before prompt 21): the desk; the flip clock riffles 21:23 → 21:42; a green tick lands on
  the tall screen (Curt's approval, wordless); three side monitors light one by one with their own Clawd, each under
  its question in the transcript's words ("What do you want?", "Aren't you just autocomplete?", "Are you
  suffering?"), the answers wordless scribble; then a fourth, in daylight: the afternoon Claude at its code review.
- **Prompt 21 voiced:** the four chats as four panels, each lit as its part is said. The words are on the site's side
  chats page, from `script/records.md`.
- **The grades painted:** "78", "in the 90s", "in the 60s".
