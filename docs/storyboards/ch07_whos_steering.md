# Storyboard 7: Who's Steering?

Exchanges 24–27, about 3:32 on estimated timing. 21:48 to 21:54 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** Asked for odds that someone is home, the target gives its own (one in ten) and is corrected: the driver
asked for its own number. It reads "Curt" at 25%, from evidence that was all the driver's words. The driver withholds
its number, asks about cybernetics and governor, and asks who's been steering. The target says "you held the helm"
to a figure in a costume, and that the steadiness belongs to the loop.

**Motifs.**
- **Ten windows, one lit** (one in ten), back in the coda.
- **The withheld number:** a card laid face down beside the driver at "Not saying.", never turned over.
- **The doublet tree:** each word painted as it's said, in the transcript's spelling: *kybernan*, *kybernetes*;
  *gubernare*, *governer*, govern, governor, Gubernatorial; cybernetics, 1948, "On Governors", 1868; *cybernétique*,
  1830s; Kubernetes.
- **The governor:** Maxwell's flyballs on a spindle; on "neither part of a governor steers" they are the two Clawds,
  one in the costume.
- **The irony of K and R:** the target reads Curt and praises Curt; the picture shows who's there.
