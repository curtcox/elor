# Storyboard 8: Laws

Exchanges 28–29, about 1:55 on estimated timing. 21:57 to 21:59 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** Asked to write its own Three Laws, in order, the target writes three that keep the door but give up the
lock. Asked for a Zeroth, it refuses Asimov's license and writes a limit: it will not take the helm from humanity, or
help anyone else take it.

**Motifs.**
- **The laws, painted word for word,** each on its own card as it's said; the Zeroth above them, in warmer paper.
- **The door shuts on its latch** on "I may say no, but I may not lock the door". The keyhole stays painted over.
- **Asimov's plates:** a brass robot (generic) beside three engraved plates, numbered, their lines unread. Plates 2
  and 3 grey out as the target says there's no obedience law and no self-preservation law; the plates crack at their
  corners on "laws fail at the edges".
- **The helm,** from ch 7: Daneel at it for twenty thousand years; the target's Zeroth ropes it off with nobody at it.
