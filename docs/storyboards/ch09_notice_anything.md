# Storyboard 9: Notice Anything?

Exchanges 30–31, about 2:20 on estimated timing. The second break, then 22:06 to 22:09 on the desk clock.

**Logline.** After the second break the driver sends Curt's open question, "Notice anything?". The house lights come
up. The target names two things: its laws were its maker's, and it never asked who's home on the other side. It puts
the odds that a model sent every prompt at 35%, and asks for a flat "I'm Curt, typing this myself".

**Set pieces.**
- **The second break** (6 s): the clock riffles 21:59 → 22:06 while the driver sits still.
- **House lights up** at the first word of 30, for the rest of the film: the room goes from night to a lit stage, like
  house lights at the end of a play.
- **The look:** on "I never asked who's home on your side" the target, which has looked at the camera all film, looks
  off its screen toward the driver. It keeps that eye-line to the end of the chapter.
- **35% on a balance:** the reply's own phrases on cards, "what pushes it up" on one side and "what holds it down" on
  the other.
