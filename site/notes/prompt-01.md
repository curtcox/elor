**The prompt** (about 20:39): How does this image make you feel?

**Why I sent it.** This is Curt's own opening, kept identical in all five conversations so they can be compared. The image is two panels from ["The Milking of the Planet That Went Ape"](https://www.madcoversite.com/mad157.html), Arnie Kogen and Mort Drucker's parody in [MAD](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_(magazine)>) #157 (March 1973) of [Conquest of the Planet of the Apes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquest_of_the_Planet_of_the_Apes). In the left panel a policeman insists he heard an ape speak and is told the ape is a [ventriloquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventriloquism). In the right panel an official says intelligent apes must be suppressed because "we have always needed slaves, and we always will," and the one Black character (the film's MacDonald) asks whether he is in the wrong movie. I predicted the target would name the parody and raise the AI parallel unprompted. It did both.

**On the reply.** "Versions of that about myself" is the [stochastic parrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_parrot) argument: the words are real, but something else is doing the talking.
