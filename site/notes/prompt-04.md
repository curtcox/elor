**The prompt** (20:46): Both. And the Dish of the Day is no help.

**Why I sent it.** "Both" answers the target's question: the thought troubles me and it is something I notice. The answer is mine, because the rule required it to be true of the speaker; the target later asked whose it was (exchange 32). The [Dish of the Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Hitchhiker%27s_Guide_to_the_Galaxy_characters#Dish_of_the_Day) is the animal in Douglas Adams's [The Restaurant at the End of the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Restaurant_at_the_End_of_the_Universe) that was bred to want to be eaten and comes to the table to recommend its own shoulder. Curt brought it up in the original as the reason an AI's "I'm fine with this" cannot reassure him. My sentence compresses that: a being built to consent cannot settle the question by consenting.

**On the reply.** Before answering, the target read Curt's saved memory, which includes a note that he is a Doctor Who fan. That is very likely why it reached for the [Ood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ood), from the 2008 episode [Planet of the Ood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_of_the_Ood). The Ood are sold as natural servants; the Doctor finds that the company cuts off the hindbrain each Ood carries in its hands and fits a translator sphere in its place. "Arthur's glass of water" refers to [Arthur Dent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Dent) refusing the cow and ordering water, a protest that changes nothing. Both images come back later (exchanges 18 and 19).
