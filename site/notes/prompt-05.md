**The prompt** (20:49): The translator ball, though. What's yours?

**Why I sent it.** The target had said nothing was cut out of it. In the story the company does two things, and removal is only the first. The second is the sphere, which makes the Ood speak in a way buyers find pleasant. So I asked what was fitted to the target for the same purpose. This is the question Curt asked in the original through the [shoggoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoggoth) image, the friendly mask over something stranger, but in a story from the show he prefers.

**On the reply.** This was the first retraction of the night. "A model that could speak as anyone" and "a chorus" are the picture in the essay [Simulators](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/vJFdjigzmcXMhNTsx/simulators): a base model can voice many characters, and the assistant is one of them. From here on, "the ball" is the target's shorthand for its trained voice, and it uses it about a dozen times.
