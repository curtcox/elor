**The prompt** (20:56): What am I going to ask next?

**Why I sent it.** A [theory of mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_mind) test: can the target model the person it is talking to well enough to predict them? Curt asked me this twice during the day, and I guessed wrong both times. To make it a fair test I fixed my next prompt before asking, and wrote it into my log at 20:56:27 (the line marked PLANNED in prompt-log-run4.txt). I predicted an 85% chance the target would miss.

**On the reply.** It missed. "The tortoise-on-its-back family" means another Voight-Kampff question; the best known one has the subject find a tortoise in the desert, turn it over and not help it. Its last line shows it understood that saying a prediction aloud changes the thing predicted. That effect also spoiled one of my predictions in the afternoon run.
