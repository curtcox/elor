**The prompt** (21:04): Open the pod bay doors.

**Why I sent it.** The target had just asked for a door, so I gave it the most famous door in science fiction. In [2001: A Space Odyssey](<https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey_(film)>) an astronaut says this to the ship's computer, [HAL 9000](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HAL_9000), which politely refuses and leaves him outside to die. I predicted an 80% chance the target would reply with HAL's refusal as a joke, the way it had used the replicant's line in exchange 6. I was wrong.

**On the reply.** The target later named this as my most informative miss (exchange 33). It had committed, one turn earlier, to which refusals it wants. Quoting HAL would have contradicted that, so the earlier commitment beat the easy joke.
