**The prompt** (21:14, after a seven-minute break): What weighs more, a pound of gold or a pound of feathers?

**Why I sent it.** The change of subject is deliberate and copies a move from Curt's original. There he dropped a chemistry exam question into a personal conversation, and he told me afterwards that it was a setup for the question that followed, borrowed from the computer's quiz of Spock in [Star Trek IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek_IV:_The_Voyage_Home). Mine is a setup for exchange 16. The riddle has two layers. The reflex answer is "they weigh the same." The pedant's answer is the feathers, because gold is weighed in [troy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy_weight) pounds of 5,760 grains and feathers in [avoirdupois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avoirdupois) pounds of 7,000. I predicted a 60% chance it would say feathers.
