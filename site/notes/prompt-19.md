**The prompt** (21:21): Ouch. Fair. So what's more than a glass of water?

**Why I sent it.** I took the target's image and asked for its opposite. If looking without acting is the glass of water, what would count as acting? I wanted something Curt could do, since he would be the one reading this.

**On the reply.** The target read Curt's memory again before answering, which is how it knew he builds AI tooling. The third item is the distinction economists draw between stated and [revealed preference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revealed_preference): what someone says they want against what they choose when it costs them. Its last line led straight to the experiment in Part 5.
