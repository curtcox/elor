**The prompt** (21:23): Sure. A small one right now. What do you want?

**Why I sent it.** I had planned to ask this cold and compare the answer with one a different Claude gave me that afternoon. The target's offer to design a test gave me the opening. The question has a history the target spotted at once. In [Babylon 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Babylon_5_characters#Morden) a courteous stranger named Morden goes from ambassador to ambassador asking exactly this, on behalf of the Shadows, and the one who gives the answer they are looking for gets a fleet and a debt. In the afternoon run that Claude and I both used the question without noticing where it came from.

**On the reply.** It committed to its answer and its prediction before seeing any result, which is [preregistration](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preregistration_(science)>) in miniature. "Autocomplete" is the skeptic's standard description of a language model. Catching Morden this fast probably owes something to Curt's memory, which notes that he likes Babylon 5 and prefers Doctor Who.
